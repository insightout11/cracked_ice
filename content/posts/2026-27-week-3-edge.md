# Week 3 Edge: a tough week to stream, so go in with a plan

Last week alternated: quiet night, busy night, repeat. This week doesn't. Every team in the league plays Tuesday, and Thursday and Saturday are nearly as full. 41 of the week's 50 games land on those three nights.

That makes streaming harder. A pickup who plays Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday will mostly sit behind the players you already have. The games that count are on Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday, and only two teams play more than one of them. So this is a week to decide your moves before Monday, not on the fly.

Tuesday is the fullest night there can be: every team plays, so every player you own has a game. A pickup only starts on Tuesday if he's better than someone already in your lineup. Here's how the teams split:

- **No off-nights:** 15 teams play Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday only, and Los Angeles plays just Tuesday and Saturday.

## Fill your D and your goalies this week

Some weeks you can get away with carrying an extra forward and running a D or a goalie short. Not this one. With every team playing Tuesday and almost every team playing Thursday and Saturday, all your D and both goalie spots will have someone available to start on the three biggest nights. An empty D or G slot on those nights is a start you don't get back, and there's no quiet-night trick that makes up for it.

So before you stream anyone, check that you have four D and two goalies who are healthy and playing. If you're a body short at either, that's your first add, ahead of any forward below.

One goalie worth a look: **Dylan Garand** (NYR, 34%). Igor Shesterkin is out for the season with a lower-body injury and Joonas Korpisalo is on IR too, so Garand is the Rangers' goalie now. He's started once and stopped 97% of the shots he's seen in two appearances, which is a tiny sample. The Rangers play Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday this week, and six times in the next two weeks. Goalie slots don't fill up the way skater slots do, so the busy nights don't hurt him.

## The strategies

![The two-add stream: Colorado Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday, then Carolina Saturday and Sunday](/blog-assets/week-2026-10-12-chain.png)

### One add: grab a Wild player

Minnesota is the only team with four games and two of them on quiet nights (Monday and Sunday). That's about 2.5 usable games for a forward and 3.1 for a D, the best of any team this week. The Wild also have a strong two weeks (8 games) and month (15 games), so this add doesn't have to be a one-week rental.

- **MIN:** Eriksson Ek (57%, 3.5 power-play minutes a game), Coleman (17%, new from Calgary), Spurgeon (14%, D, 5 points in 5 games on 22 minutes a night), Määttä (0%, D, new from Calgary and playing 21.4 minutes, up 4.2 from last season)

A caution on **Hartman** (11%), who was on last week's holds list: his ice time is down about four minutes a game from last season. I'd look at the others first.

### Two adds: Colorado Tue/Wed/Fri, then Carolina Sat/Sun

Colorado gives you Wednesday and Friday, and Carolina gives you Sunday. Together that's about 3.2 usable games for one forward spot, against 2.5 for the single Minnesota add.

- **COL:** Nelson (75%), Kadri (66%, 3.6 power-play minutes a game), Toews (47%, D, 5 points in 5 games), Landeskog (34%, 2.8 power-play minutes a game), Malinski (26%, D), Schwartz (9%)
- **CAR:** Ehlers (84%), Jackson Blake (49%), K'Andre Miller (28%, D), Hall (13%, 5 points in 6 games)

Two of those Avalanche names come with a warning. **Nelson** has 5 points in 5 games, but his power-play time is down to 1.5 minutes a game from 3.3 last season and his ice time is down 2.5 minutes. **Schwartz** has 6 points in 5 games while playing only 13 minutes a night. Both are scoring more than their roles say they should, so don't pay for the points.

### Three adds: start with a Devil on Monday

If your league gives you three moves, add a New Jersey player for Monday, then run the Colorado and Carolina stream. That covers all four quiet nights and gets you about 4.2 usable games. Unlike last week, the third add earns a full extra game, so it's worth making if you have it.

- **NJD (Monday):** Hamilton (84%, D), Evangelista (46%, new from Nashville and getting 4.9 power-play minutes a game), Mantha (17%, new from Pittsburgh)

### Spare bench spot: an Av and a Wild

Carry one of each and start whoever plays. Colorado goes Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday; Minnesota goes Monday, Tuesday, Thursday and Sunday. Between them there's a game every night but Saturday, about 4.5 that fit a lineup.

### Save an add if you can

In a week like this, a spare add is worth more than usual. The quiet nights are at the edges of the week (Monday and Sunday) and in the gaps (Wednesday and Friday), so a late move for a Sunday player, or to replace someone who gets hurt, can swing a matchup. If your third or fourth add would only buy a Tuesday or Saturday game, keep it.

## Teams to trim this week

![Teams to trim this week: Los Angeles and Anaheim play twice, and 15 more teams have no quiet nights](/blog-assets/week-2026-10-12-trim.png)

If you need to drop someone to make a move, start here.

- **Los Angeles** plays twice, Tuesday and Saturday, the two fullest nights of the week. A fringe King gets you about 0.3 usable games. He's this week's easiest drop.
- **Anaheim** also plays only twice (Tuesday and Friday). Friday helps, but it's still two games.
- **The 16 teams with no off-nights:** Chicago, Detroit, Edmonton, Nashville, both New York teams, Philadelphia, Seattle, San Jose, St. Louis, Tampa Bay, Toronto, Utah, Vancouver and Winnipeg all play Tuesday, Thursday and Saturday only, and Los Angeles makes sixteen. Their stars still start for you. Their depth players are the ones who'll sit, so a marginal player from one of these teams is a reasonable drop for a Wild or Avalanche player.

Don't drop anyone good because of one bad week. This is about the last spot or two on your bench.

## How this works

- I drafted 1,800 fantasy rosters (150 simulated 12-team, 16-round Yahoo drafts) off ADP, with some noise and a few deep picks.
- Yahoo standard skater slots: 2 C, 2 LW, 2 RW, 4 D. Each night, a roster's playing skaters fill what they can, and a pickup's game counts only if he fills one more slot. That's a usable game.
- Everything is in games, not points, because every league scores differently. The games that fit a lineup don't change.
- Ownership is Yahoo's percent owned this week. That's an average across leagues, not your league, which is why every team gets options at several levels.
- New this week: once a player has three games this season, his ice time and power-play time are compared with last season's. Scoring pace and second-half splits still come from last season until he has 20 games. Where this season's role is clearly smaller than last season's, I say so.

None of this knows who's free in your league or what your scoring rewards. It just tells you where the games that count are.
