# Week 2 Edge: every other night is wide open

Week 1's in the books, and thanks to everyone who read, shared and argued with last week's post. You asked for it every week, so here we go.

Week 2 has a rhythm to it. Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday are quiet, Tuesday and Thursday are busy, and Saturday is packed. If you stream on the quiet nights and ignore the rest, you'll be fine. Here's the full version, with every team worth targeting this week, over the next two weeks and over the next month.

## The strategies

### Two adds: Ottawa Mon/Tue, then Washington Wed/Fri/Sun

Ottawa opens the week Monday and Tuesday, and Washington plays three of the four quiet nights after that. That's about 4.4 usable games for one forward spot. The best single add (a Penguin, below) gets about 3.0.

Kyrou's new in Washington, so check his line and his power-play spot from their first two games before you pick him over Strome or Leonard.

### A third add? Skip it

You can squeeze a third add in (Ottawa, then Colorado Wednesday and Thursday, then the Rangers Friday and Sunday), but it only gets you about half a game more than the two-add stream. Save the move.

### One add: grab a Penguin

Four games, three of them on quiet nights, and Pittsburgh also has the best schedule over the next two weeks (7 games, 4.2 usable). If you only want to make one move, this is it.

The interesting name is **Chinakhov** (33%). He had 6 points in 29 games in Columbus last season, then 36 in 43 after the trade to Pittsburgh on New Year's Day. Bryan Rust is on IR, and Chinakhov played 17 minutes in the opener with 5 of them on the power play. One game isn't a trend, but that's the role you want. Letang (13%, D) is the deep option.

### Spare bench spot: rotate a Penguin and a Hurricane

Carry one of each and start whoever plays. Pittsburgh goes Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Saturday, Carolina goes Tuesday, Thursday, Saturday and Sunday, so between them there's a game every night. That's 7 games and about 5 that fit a lineup, with only Saturday overlapping.

- **CAR:** Jackson Blake (46%, 7.7 power-play minutes in the opener with Jarvis out), K'Andre Miller (31%, D), Hall (10%, finished hot: 0.44 points a game before New Year's, 0.76 after)

## How this works

- I drafted 1,800 fantasy rosters (150 simulated 12-team, 16-round Yahoo drafts) off ADP, with some noise and a few deep picks.
- Yahoo standard skater slots: 2 C, 2 LW, 2 RW, 4 D. Each night, a roster's playing skaters fill what they can, and a pickup's game counts only if he fills one more slot. That's a usable game.
- Everything is in games, not points, because every league scores differently. The games that fit a lineup don't change.
- Ownership is Yahoo's percent owned this week. That's an average across leagues, not your league, which is why every team gets options at several levels.
- Player notes come from last season until a player has 20 games this season: second-half splits (at New Year's), power-play time (never for players on a new team), youth, new teams and injured teammates. Opening-week ice time shows up where it says something about a role, but one game is one game.

None of this knows who's free in your league or what your scoring rewards. It just tells you where the games that count are.
