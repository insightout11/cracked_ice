# Best position group stacks for fantasy hockey

When two or more players compete for the same fantasy slots, their team schedules determine how many games can reach your active lineup. A third center who often plays on the same nights as your first two centers may spend more time on the bench than a comparable player whose team fills quieter dates.

## What makes a useful position stack

- Strong enough per-game production for the acquisition cost.
- Fewer shared game dates within the position group.
- More games on off-nights.
- Eligibility that can move between active slots.
- A schedule that still works during your fantasy playoffs.

The schedule cannot predict injuries, role changes, or performance. It can quantify opportunity. Use that information as a tiebreaker after accounting for player quality and league scoring.
