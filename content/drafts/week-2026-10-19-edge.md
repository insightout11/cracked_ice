# Week 4 Edge: four quiet nights, and Colorado plays three

Last week was a grind. Every team played Tuesday, and almost every pickup sat on the nights that mattered. This week is friendlier. Monday, Wednesday, Friday and Sunday are all quiet, and on each of them at least nine rosters in ten have room for another forward.

The best part: one team plays three of those four nights. If you only make one move this week, it's an Avalanche player.

## The strategies

![The two-add stream: Philadelphia Monday and Wednesday, then Montreal Friday and Sunday](/blog-assets/week-2026-10-19-chain.png)

### One add: grab an Avalanche player

Colorado plays Monday, Tuesday, Friday and Sunday. Three of those are quiet nights, so a Colorado forward gets you about 3.1 usable games, and a Colorado D about 3.4. Nobody else is close; the next best single add is 2.2. And they stay on top over two weeks too (6 games, 4.6 usable).

- **COL:** Kadri (66%, 4.0 power-play minutes a game), Landeskog (34%, 5 points in 8), Malinski (20%, D), Schwartz (14%)

Kadri is the one to grab if he's there. He's playing 20 minutes a night, up a minute from last season, with four minutes on the power play and three shots a game. In a lot of leagues he won't be, so Landeskog is the realistic forward.

Two cautions. **Schwartz** has 6 points in 8 games, but he's playing 13.2 minutes a night, down almost three minutes from his Seattle days. That's fourth-line ice time scoring like a second liner, and it rarely lasts. **Malinski** is a useful body for the D spot (Colorado's D get the most usable games of anyone this week), but he has one point in seven games and his ice time is down a bit. Take him for the games, not the upside.

### Two adds: Philadelphia Mon/Wed, then Montreal Fri/Sun

This one covers all four quiet nights. A Flyer gets you Monday and Wednesday, then you swap to a Canadien for Friday and Sunday. That's about 3.8 usable games for one forward spot, against 3.1 for the single Colorado add.

- **PHI:** Zegras (60%), Michkov (27%), Sanheim (33%, D, 25 minutes a night), Drysdale (5%, D, 2.7 power-play minutes a game)
- **MTL:** Kreider (23%), Newhook (2%), Texier (0%)

Philadelphia's forward group is thin right now. Travis Konecny is on IR and Owen Tippett is out, so Michkov's role should grow. He's at 3.0 power-play minutes a game already, up 0.8. Zegras is the better player but has 4 points in 10 games, and his ownership has dropped 12 points this week. That might be your window.

Montreal's options are thinner. Kreider is on the power play (3.0 minutes a game) but his ice time is down two minutes from last season in Anaheim. Ivan Demidov is on IR and Mike Matheson has been day-to-day with a lower-body injury, so this is a schedule play more than a talent play. If you'd rather have a stronger name for Friday and Sunday, a Colorado player plays those same two nights. You'd give up nothing in usable games.

### Three adds: Toronto, Ottawa, then Montreal

If you've got three moves, the best path is a Leaf for Monday and Tuesday, a Senator for Wednesday and Thursday, then a Canadien for Friday and Sunday. About 4.4 usable games.

- **TOR (Mon/Tue):** Roslovic (24%, 6 points in 9, 3.3 shots a game), Rielly (36%, D)
- **OTT (Wed/Thu):** Giroux (21%, 3.5 power-play minutes a game, up 1.3), Pinto (21%), Spence (2%, D, 22.4 minutes a night, up 3.7)

John Tavares is on IR as of Saturday, which should mean more minutes for Roslovic. In Ottawa, check the news before you commit: captain Thomas Chabot left Saturday's game against the Flyers with an upper-body injury, and Jake Sanderson has been out since opening night (he may be back Wednesday). If both are out, Spence's 22 minutes go up again.

Honestly, the third add only buys you about 0.6 more usable games over the two-add stream. If you're low on moves, it's the one to skip.

### Spare bench spot: a Senator and an Av

Carry one of each and start whoever plays. Colorado goes Monday, Tuesday, Friday and Sunday; Ottawa goes Wednesday, Thursday and Saturday. That's a game every night of the week, seven in all, and about 4.6 that fit a lineup.

## How this works

- I drafted 1,800 fantasy rosters (150 simulated 12-team, 16-round Yahoo drafts) off ADP, with some noise and a few deep picks.
- Yahoo standard skater slots: 2 C, 2 LW, 2 RW, 4 D. Each night, a roster's playing skaters fill what they can, and a pickup's game counts only if he fills one more slot. That's a usable game.
- Everything is in games, not points, because every league scores differently.
- Ownership is Yahoo's percent owned this week. That's an average across leagues, not your league, which is why every team gets options at several levels.
- Ice time and power-play time are this season's, compared with last season's, once a player has three games. Stats are through Saturday, Oct 17.

None of this knows who's free in your league or what your scoring rewards. It just tells you where the games that count are.
