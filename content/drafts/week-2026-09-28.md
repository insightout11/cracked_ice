# Week 1 Edge: Saturday has 13 games and almost no room

Most weekly schedule posts count games. This one counts the games that actually fit in a lineup.

For every week this season, Cracked Ice drafts 1,800 realistic fantasy rosters from Yahoo draft positions, sets each roster's best lineup every night, and measures when a pickup could actually play. A game on a night when your forwards are already full is worth nothing.

## The week in one picture

![Week 1 at a glance: games per night and the share of rosters with an open forward slot](/blog-assets/week-2026-09-28-glance.png)

Opening week runs Tuesday to Sunday (Monday has no games). Five of the six game nights are light, and on those nights 72% to 95% of rosters have an open forward slot.

Then there's Saturday: **13 games, and only 21% of rosters have room for another forward.** Anything you add for Saturday will probably sit on your bench.

Looking ahead, Winnipeg is the only team playing both Sunday and next Monday, the one bridge into Week 2.

## Games vs starts that count

![Games vs usable starts per team for Week 1](/blog-assets/week-2026-09-28-starts.png)

The Rangers and Canucks both play four games. For a forward pickup, the Rangers are worth **3.4 usable starts** and the Canucks **2.7**, because Vancouver's fourth game lands on packed Saturday.

The same thing happens across the three-game teams. Vegas and Florida play all three of their games on light nights (2.7 and 2.5 usable starts). Chicago, Edmonton, Calgary, Philadelphia, Seattle and Utah each have one game on Saturday, so their three games are worth about 1.9.

## One open slot, planned

![One-slot stream plan and a two-player rotation for Week 1](/blog-assets/week-2026-09-28-plan.png)

If you have one open forward spot, this is the best plan among rarely drafted forwards (Yahoo average pick after 150, or undrafted), scored in Yahoo standard points and weighted by how often a roster has room that night:

1. **Ivan Barbashev (VGK)** for Tuesday.
2. **Noah Cates (PHI)** for Wednesday and Thursday.
3. **Viktor Arvidsson (DET)** for Friday and Sunday.

That's about **31.6 expected points over three adds**. The best single pickup held all week, Will Cuylle (NYR), is worth about 24.2.

**If your league limits adds,** carry Cuylle and Cates together and start whoever plays: about **31.4 expected points with two adds**. It does use a spare bench spot.

These are schedule and lineup math, not availability claims. Check who's actually free in your league.

## How this is calculated

- **Rosters:** 150 simulated 12-team, 16-round Yahoo drafts (1,800 rosters). Picks follow Yahoo average draft position with noise, a few deep-league picks of NHL regulars, and at most three goalies per team.
- **Lineups:** Yahoo standard skater slots (2 C, 2 LW, 2 RW, 4 D). Each night, a roster's playing skaters are placed to fill as many slots as possible. A pickup "fits" if adding him fills one more.
- **Scoring:** last full season, Yahoo standard points per game, as a reference rate.
- **Light and packed nights:** 8 or fewer, and 13 or more, NHL games.
- Injured players listed as out are left off the simulated rosters and the candidates.
